Questions

Before you book

How much does a wedding dress cost?

Gowns from our collection are USD 980 – 1,680. A gown designed and made to your measurements is USD 1,580 – 1,980. The price depends on fabric, beadwork and construction.

Can I buy a dress while I am travelling in Vietnam?

Yes. Book your fitting before you fly. We shortlist gowns for your date and measurements, fit you in person and adjust the dress to you.

Do you ship overseas?

We don't charge a shipping fee. You can carry the dress home, or send it with a courier of your choice — shipping is arranged and paid by you directly.

Is there a fee to try on dresses?

Your first 5 gowns are free to try. From the 6th gown there is a fee of 100,000 VND per gown. Fittings are private and must be booked ahead.

Which city should I choose?

Whichever is closer to your wedding or your trip. Both studios offer private fittings — tell us your city when you book.
